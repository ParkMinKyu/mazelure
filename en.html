ABOUT

A lost hero who fights just fine alone

Sorties, battles and the next run all happen automatically. Check the results and swap your gear.

Idle farming you just leave running

Your hero roams the maze, picks up treasure, slays monsters and goes after the boss. Win and move on to the next run; lose and stop or try again.

  • Auto sortie · auto battle · auto progress
  • Turn on Clairvoyance, Compass, Goddess's Blessing and Master's Whim and they're used for you
  • Estimated win rate shows at a glance whether your gear can win
  • Even while you're away, the Labor Camp and Mercenaries keep collecting rewards

A race against the boss

The boss is awake from the start. It devours minions and swallows hidden legendaries to grow stronger — farm faster than it does.

  • Hero and boss HP, win rate and boss info right on screen
  • Trap — drop walls around the boss to pin it down
  • Five boss gimmicks: Regen · Thorns · Ambush · Lifesteal · Crit

Collect, enhance, dive deeper

Six boss sets and six legendary weapons. Gather copies to enhance them and find the combo that counters each boss gimmick.

  • Clear the five-act campaign, then dive into the never-ending Endless
  • Up to +25 in one go at the Refinery, then gold enhancement and Enhance Tickets — legendaries go up to +50
  • Endless overall · per-weapon · Power rankings

One scroll turns you into a different hero

Buy a transformation scroll at the Magic Shop and switch it on before a sortie — you fight that one run as that class.

CONTENTS

Solo, and together

Grow stronger through the campaign and Endless, then claim rewards from raids and shared content.

Adventure

Five-act campaign

A long expedition through eight bosses in a row per act. Clear Act 5 to unlock Endless.

Endless

Bosses grow stronger and the maze wider the deeper you go. Boss gimmicks (Regen · Thorns · Ambush · Lifesteal · Crit) change every 5 floors, and from floor 151 every win earns Royal Funding.

Bounty raid

A fresh bounty every day. Race a boss that's awake from the start for the legendary in the middle of the map.

Raids

Crystal Golem raid

Defeat three bosses to break the seal and strike the golem. Get knocked back and a stronger boss reseals it — how many waves can you last?

Survival

Wipe out a wave and the next one swarms in. Pick a class, and climb the rankings by the wave you reach. Unlocks at Endless floor 11.

Party Battle

Pick two companions and fight your way up through six rooms. Defeat each room's boss to open the seal above — the golem waits at the top. Unlocks at Endless floor 51.

Together

World Boss

Every signed-in adventurer hits the same boss. Your Power is your damage, and progress pays out gold and Enhance Tickets.

Slime Race

Pick the winner among five racers. Pay the entry fee in legendary copies; guess right for skill points and an Enhance Ticket. 10 races a day.

Shared Altar · Gold Altar

When everyone's offerings fill the altar gauge, everyone gets a bonus. The higher the Gold Altar's tier, the higher the Refinery success rate.

Rewards that pile up

Labor Camp · Familiar

Monsters you defeat craft materials and fragments in the Labor Camp. Make an offering to your Familiar and its blessing stays with you all day.

Mercenaries

Form a band of Mage, Paladin and Rogue mercenaries with transformation scrolls and send them out — a few hours later they return with a bag of gear.

Daily Missions · Achievements

Five missions a day plus a completion bonus. Achievement points reduce the copies needed for Transcend and ticket exchange, and get you Magic Shop discounts.

Growth

Forge · Refinery

Gather copies to craft and enhance. Up to +25 in one go at the Refinery, then gold enhancement and Enhance Tickets — legendaries reach +50. Spare copies go into Transcend.

Magic Shop

Transformation scrolls, class skills, and [Research] for special skills.

Rankings

Endless (overall · per weapon) · Power · Survival rankings. Sign in for cloud backup and pick up where you left off on any device.
